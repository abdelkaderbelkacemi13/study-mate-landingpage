StudyMate

Your Ultimate Companion for Learning and Mastery

StudyMate is a platform where teachers and students can master skills and achieve goals. This initial version allows teachers to easily distribute study materials and homework, while students can access and learn from them.

Try StudyMate

Features

Student Dashboard

Access all your study materials anytime, anywhere. Submit homework easily and stay organized.

Teacher Dashboard

Upload study materials and homework in advance, saving valuable class time for instruction and discussion.

Quizzes

Test your knowledge with updated quizzes. This is just one of the many features we're developing.

Group chats

Each teacher has the ability to create group chats to his students, where they can work together and share knowledge.

Live sessions

teacher can do live sessions to teach.

Online Exams

Tests and Exams can be done Online
